Volume 1 · Chapter 2

The Day the Sky Opened

A literary condensed recap

The first sign was a pressure in the air.

It came so suddenly that the ordinary noises of Shiganshina seemed to hesitate. A wagon wheel rattled over stone. Someone laughed from an upstairs window. Then a violent gust rushed through the street, warm and unnatural, flinging dust into people's eyes and sending birds into frantic circles above the rooftops.

Eren turned toward the wall.

For a heartbeat he could not understand what he was seeing.

Something had risen beyond Wall Maria.

A head. Shoulders. Steam drifting from exposed muscle. The wall, which had always erased the horizon, suddenly looked small beneath the thing standing behind it.

People stopped moving.

The district had spent a century imagining Titans as monsters that belonged to the other side. Their danger was real, but distant. The wall transformed terror into history, something parents warned children about and soldiers discussed over drinks.

Now history was looking down at them.

Eren felt his mouth go dry.

All his resentment toward the walls remained somewhere inside him, but another truth arrived beside it: people had trusted these stones because they were afraid of what waited outside.

The fear had been justified.

• • •

The giant moved.

The blow struck the gate with such force that the street seemed to jump beneath Eren's feet. Stone burst outward. Timber shattered. A cloud of debris climbed into the sky and rolled through the district like a storm.

For several seconds there was only sound.

Then came the screaming.

People ran without knowing where the danger was. Some rushed toward home, others toward the inner gate. Parents shouted children's names. Soldiers who had been lounging at their posts moments earlier began calling orders over one another, their voices stripped of all boredom.

The breach stood open.

Beyond it lay the land humanity had hidden from for generations.

Movement appeared through the dust.

Titans were coming in.

Eren watched the first distant shapes pass through the broken gate, and an impossible feeling seized him. He had dreamed all his life of the world outside. He had imagined exploration, freedom, the ocean Armin described.

He had never imagined the outside world entering his home.

• • •

Mikasa found him before panic could.

They looked at one another, and the same thought passed between them.

Carla.

They ran.

The crowd flowed in the opposite direction, turning every street into resistance. Eren shoved between adults and ducked beneath arms while Mikasa stayed close behind. Dust had turned the sunlight gray. Roof tiles lay broken underfoot. Somewhere nearby a building groaned and collapsed, sending another wave of people into flight.

They heard reports as they ran. Titans inside the district. Soldiers falling back. The gate destroyed.

Eren refused to let the words become images.

His mother was home. That was the only fact that mattered.

He pushed forward until his lungs burned.

Every familiar street looked changed by fear. A bakery he knew had lost its front wall. A cart had overturned in an intersection. A man stood calling a name into the smoke while everyone moved around him.

Eren had spent his childhood believing Shiganshina was too small.

Now it seemed impossibly large.

• • •

When they reached the Yeager neighborhood, Eren slowed.

A massive section of debris had fallen across the houses.

For a moment he looked past the wreckage, searching for the shape his memory expected: the roof, the windows, the door his mother would open.

None of it was where it should have been.

Then Mikasa saw her.

Carla was trapped beneath the remains of the house.

She was alive.

Eren ran to her so quickly that he nearly fell.

Relief came first. He heard her voice. He saw her looking at him. For one foolish instant that was enough to make him believe the situation could still be repaired.

Then he saw the timber across her lower body.

He grabbed it.

Mikasa joined him.

Together they pulled until their arms shook.

The beam did not move.

Carla told them to leave. Eren acted as if he had not heard.

He changed his grip and tried again.

Somewhere beyond the ruined houses, something heavy touched the ground.

The impact traveled through the street.

Another step followed.

Slow.

Measured.

Eren kept pulling.

The sound came closer.

Mikasa looked toward the end of the street.

Her face changed.

Eren followed her eyes.

A Titan was walking toward them.
