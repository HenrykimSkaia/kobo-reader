Volume 1 · Chapter 6

The Enemy Returns

A condensed prose retelling

The morning at Trost began with ordinary work.

The newly graduated cadets stood atop the wall, maintaining equipment and talking about the choices ahead of them. For the first time in years, Eren could see a future that felt close enough to touch.

Then the air changed.

A burst of heat rolled across the wall.

Steam exploded upward.

Eren turned and found himself staring once again at the nightmare that had divided his life into before and after.

The Colossal Titan.

• • •

Five years disappeared inside a heartbeat.

Eren remembered falling stone. Smoke. His mother's voice. The retreat from Shiganshina.

But he was no longer a child standing helpless in the street.

He drew his blades and moved.

The training that had once felt repetitive now became instinct. Gas hissed through the maneuvering gear. Cables snapped into stone. Eren launched himself toward the towering figure while the other soldiers scrambled to understand what was happening.

This time, he intended to fight back.

For a moment, the enormous Titan seemed impossibly close. Eren saw steam spilling from its body and believed he had an opening.

Then the creature vanished.

Only heat and vapor remained.

There was no victory to celebrate.

The damage had already been done.

• • •

Below them, chaos spread through Trost.

The gate had been breached. Titans entered the district, wandering among buildings and toward the fleeing population.

Orders came quickly. The newly graduated soldiers were no longer students deciding which branch of the military suited them best. They were combatants.

Some tried to hide their fear. Some failed.

Jean stared toward the streets below and understood that every argument about comfort and promotion had belonged to another world. Connie's usual lightness disappeared. Armin's face tightened as he calculated dangers faster than he could emotionally accept them.

Mikasa was assigned elsewhere.

Eren hated that immediately.

He had spent years preparing for this moment, yet the battlefield broke apart the people he trusted and scattered them into different units.

He told himself it did not matter.

They were soldiers now.

They had trained for this.

As Eren's squad prepared to move into the city, he looked at the smoke rising between the rooftops.

The fear was still there.

But so was something stronger.

This time, he would not run.
