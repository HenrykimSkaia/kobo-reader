The First Battle

A condensed prose retelling

The first minutes of combat destroyed almost everything the cadets had imagined war would be.

Training had given them techniques. It had not prepared them for the size of a Titan at street level, for the way buildings trapped sound, or for how quickly a familiar voice could disappear.

Eren's squad moved across the rooftops toward their assigned sector.

Then the formation broke.

A Titan appeared where they did not expect one. Another blocked the route ahead. Decisions that had seemed simple during exercises became impossible when every mistake carried a human cost.

Eren saw friends fall before he had time to understand that they were gone.

• • •

Armin froze.

His body stopped responding even while his mind screamed at him to move. Terror narrowed the world to fragments: a roof edge, a shadow, an enormous hand, the open mouth of a Titan.

He was caught.

For a moment, the battlefield vanished beneath pure panic.

Then Eren came after him.

There was no strategy left, only instinct.

Eren reached Armin when escape should already have been impossible. He forced his friend away from death and paid for the choice almost immediately.

Pain shattered through him.

Armin landed beyond the Titan's grasp and turned back.

What he saw did not make sense.

Eren, who had spent years insisting that humanity could fight, was suddenly the one being taken.

• • •

Time seemed to slow.

Armin reached toward him.

Eren looked back from the darkness of the Titan's mouth.

In another life they had sat together with a forbidden book between them and imagined places neither had seen: oceans stretching beyond the horizon, fields of ice, distant lands untouched by walls.

Those dreams had once seemed childish because they were so far away.

Now they were unbearable because they might never happen.

Eren made one final effort to push Armin toward survival.

Then the Titan's jaws closed.

The street filled again with noise.

Armin remained where he had fallen, staring at the place where his friend had disappeared.

All the promises they had made about the outside world seemed to collapse at once.

Above Trost, smoke continued to rise.

The battle had barely begun.

And for the first time since they were children, Armin faced the world without Eren beside him.
