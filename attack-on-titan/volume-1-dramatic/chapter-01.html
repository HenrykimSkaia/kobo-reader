The Dream Beneath the Tree

A dramatic literary recap

Eren woke with tears drying on his cheeks and no memory of why he had been crying.

Above him, sunlight trembled through the leaves. The day was warm. The grass beneath his hands smelled green and ordinary. Nothing in the quiet suggested that the world had already begun moving toward disaster.

Mikasa stood over him with a bundle of firewood in her arms. She studied his face for a moment longer than necessary, as if she had seen something in his sleep that he had not. Eren brushed the tears away and told himself they meant nothing.

From the hill, Shiganshina looked peaceful. Red roofs crowded beneath Wall Maria, and beyond the district rose the immense stone barrier that had contained humanity for a century. Adults called it salvation. Priests called it sacred. Eren thought it looked like the rim of a cage.

He and Mikasa returned through the gate just as the Survey Corps came home.

The crowd gathered eagerly at first. Then the cheering died.

The soldiers rode in beneath torn green cloaks. Some leaned half-conscious over their saddles. Others returned without the companions who had ridden beside them that morning. A mother forced her way through the crowd, searching every face until a soldier handed her the only piece of her son that had come back.

Eren watched the commander struggle to explain that the expedition had not been meaningless. The words sounded fragile against the woman's grief. Around Eren, people muttered that the Survey Corps wasted food, lives, and taxes chasing a world no human was meant to see.

Their fear made him furious.

Failure did not prove the outside world was worthless. It proved that humanity was still too weak to reach it.

Armin understood. Hidden beneath his clothes was a forbidden book filled with impossible things: an ocean wider than any wall, fields of ice, deserts of sand, mountains that breathed fire. The pages had given shape to Eren's anger. Somewhere beyond the Titans lay a world no wall had the right to keep from him.

That evening, his mother learned that he wanted to join the Survey Corps. Her fear arrived before her words. His father listened more quietly. Before leaving on a trip, Grisha promised that when he returned, he would finally show Eren what lay inside the locked basement beneath their house.

The key at his father's throat flashed once in the lamplight.

Outside, the bells marked another peaceful hour.

High above the district, Wall Maria stood white beneath the evening sky—ancient, silent, and certain.

No one in Shiganshina knew that certainty had only minutes left to live.
