Volume 1 · Dramatic Edition · Chapter 7

The Mouth of the Titan

A dramatic literary recap

The first battle shattered the cadets' confidence.

Titans appeared from blind corners and blocked escape routes. Friends vanished before anyone could react. Armin froze when a Titan seized him and lifted him toward its mouth.

Eren attacked.

Already wounded, he reached Armin and forced him away from death. For one suspended moment, their childhood dream of seeing the ocean seemed impossibly close and impossibly far.

Eren pushed Armin to safety.

The Titan's jaws closed around him.

Armin fell onto a rooftop and stared at the place where his friend had disappeared. Around him, Trost continued to collapse. Gas supplies were running low. Soldiers were trapped. Mikasa fought elsewhere, unaware of what had happened.

Grief threatened to empty Armin's mind, but the living still needed a way out.

Below, the Titan that had swallowed Eren turned toward another victim.

And somewhere inside the nightmare, something refused to die.

This is a condensed, transformative recap. It preserves the major story progression while using original prose and does not reproduce manga dialogue or panels.
