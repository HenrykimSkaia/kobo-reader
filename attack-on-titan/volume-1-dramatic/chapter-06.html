Volume 1 · Dramatic Edition · Chapter 6

The Colossal Returns

A dramatic literary recap

For a heartbeat, Eren was a child in Shiganshina again.

Then training took control.

He launched himself toward the Colossal Titan, blades drawn, determined to strike the nape. Steam erupted from the giant's body, turning the wall into a furnace. Eren forced himself forward—but before he could attack, the Titan vanished.

The damage remained.

Trost's gate had been destroyed. Titans entered the district while civilians fled toward the inner wall. The newly graduated cadets were divided into combat units and sent into a battle none of them truly understood.

Mikasa was ordered elsewhere. Armin remained with Eren.

From the rooftops, the cadets watched Titans move between familiar houses. Confidence disappeared quickly. Training had taught them techniques; reality showed them how fast a single mistake became death.

Eren's squad descended into the streets.

Within minutes, their formation began to collapse.

This is a condensed, transformative recap. It preserves the major story progression while using original prose and does not reproduce manga dialogue or panels.
