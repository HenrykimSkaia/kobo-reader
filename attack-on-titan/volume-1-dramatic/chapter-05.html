The Cadets of the 104th

A dramatic literary recap

Years of training hardened the 104th Cadet Corps into soldiers.

Mikasa became the finest fighter among them. Armin survived through intelligence. Eren advanced by refusing to accept defeat. Around them, Jean, Reiner, Bertolt, Annie, Connie, and Sasha formed a tense circle of rivals and companions.

Graduation forced each cadet to choose between safety and danger. The Military Police offered comfort within the interior. The Garrison offered duty. The Survey Corps offered almost certain death beyond the walls.

Eren chose the Survey Corps without hesitation.

His certainty unsettled the others. Five quiet years had made disaster feel distant, but Eren remembered that the walls had already failed once.

The following morning at Trost, ordinary life continued below the battlements. Then heat and lightning split the air.

The Colossal Titan appeared above the wall.

The past had returned before any of them were ready.

This is a condensed, transformative recap. It preserves the major story progression while using original prose and does not reproduce manga dialogue or panels.
