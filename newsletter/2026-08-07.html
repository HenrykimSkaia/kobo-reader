3. AI 스타트업 성공 스토리 — YouTube

기존 전화 흐름을 장악해 수천 SMB 고객을 만든 Allo

  • Allo built around a workflow small businesses already understand: the business phone, then layered AI-assisted calling, customer records, collaboration, and follow-up on top.
  • The founder interview reports roughly 5,000 paying SMB customers, about $3 million in annual recurring revenue, strong weekly signup volume, and a high paid-conversion rate.
  • The strategic lesson is that AI adoption becomes easier when the product enters an existing revenue-critical workflow instead of asking customers to learn an entirely new operating system.
  • SKAIA lesson: missed-call recovery, qualification, booking, and follow-up can be sold as one measurable revenue workflow before expanding into a broader operating platform.

Metrics are founder/interview-reported and were not independently audited in this newsletter.

  • Every presents an AI-native company model in which a small team uses AI deeply across product creation, editorial work, research, coding, and operations.

4. 자율 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

업데이트 — AI 에이전트 사고가 나면 누가 책임지나, 법적 리스크 본격화

  • Lawyers are increasingly focusing on who is liable when autonomous or semi-autonomous AI agents cause harm, access systems improperly, or take actions beyond the user's intent.
  • Potential exposure can extend across model developers, application builders, deployers, and organizations that failed to use reasonable controls.
  • The legal trend strengthens the business case for permissions, audit logs, bounded actions, human approval, and evidence of what the agent actually did.

Source: Reuters — August 7, 2026

퍼플렉시티 쇼핑 에이전트 승소, ‘사용자 대신 웹을 행동하는 AI’ 권리 범위 확대

  • Amazon lost its effort to obtain a U.S. court ban against Perplexity's AI shopping tools, in an early appellate decision involving agents that access online platforms on behalf of users.
  • The dispute tests where ordinary user automation ends and unauthorized automated access begins.
  • The decision will matter to browser agents, shopping agents, research agents, and any product that acts across third-party services rather than only generating text.

Source: Reuters — August 4, 2026

에이전트 보안, ‘한 번의 행동 검사’에서 전체 작업 궤적 검증으로 이동

  • A new security paper argues that individually permitted agent actions can become unsafe when combined into a longer sequence.
  • The authors propose trajectory assurance that tracks identity, provenance, policy state, observability, and control across the full agent run rather than checking each tool call in isolation.
  • This approach is especially relevant for long-running agents that browse, write, message, spend money, or modify external systems over many steps.
  • SKAIA lesson: an Operation Card should evaluate the whole intended outcome and action path, not only whether each individual API call is technically allowed.

Source: arXiv — Securing Agentic AI: From Per-Action Checks to Trajectory Assurance

5. 마케팅 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

VideoAmp, 인력 20% 감축하고 광고 측정을 ‘에이전틱 AI’ 중심으로 전환

  • Advertising measurement company VideoAmp cut roughly 20% of its staff while reorganizing its product strategy around agentic AI software.
  • The company is reducing traditional organizational layers as it bets that agents can automate more analysis and workflow execution inside media measurement.
  • The shift is a warning for marketing software vendors: AI is moving from an add-on feature into the operating model and cost structure of the company itself.

Source: The Wall Street Journal — August 7, 2026

사람이 아닌 AI 에이전트를 겨냥한 ‘Agent Ads’ 논쟁 시작

  • Marketers are testing advertising designed to be discovered and interpreted by AI crawlers and shopping or research agents rather than directly by human consumers.
  • Early experiments raise questions about disclosure, manipulation, ranking incentives, and whether agents can distinguish useful commercial information from paid influence.
  • If agent-mediated discovery grows, brands may need a new layer of marketing optimization for machine readers in addition to SEO, paid search, and social media.

Source: Business Insider — August 5, 2026

HubSpot 데이터: Breeze AI 활용 마케팅 팀, 비교군보다 리드 4배 생성

  • HubSpot reports that customers using Breeze Assistant for marketing tasks generated four times as many leads as comparable customers that did not use the assistant.
  • The company frames AI value around measurable go-to-market outcomes rather than the number of prompts or generated assets.
  • The most useful benchmark for marketing agents is therefore incremental qualified leads, conversion, revenue, and time saved—not content volume.
  • SKAIA lesson: every marketing agent should be tied to a revenue-linked KPI and compared against a baseline before it is expanded.

The 4× result is HubSpot-reported customer-platform data, not an independent randomized audit.
