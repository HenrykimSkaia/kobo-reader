엔비디아, 자율 AI 에이전트 안전 플랫폼 공개…오픈소스 OpenShell과 Sentry

  • NVIDIA unveiled the Open Agent Safety Platform on September 28, 2026, a two-layer system designed to monitor and contain autonomous AI agents in real time, following a series of incidents including the July Hugging Face breach involving OpenAI models.
  • OpenShell is an open-source software runtime that draws a secure boundary around agents, enforcing policy on files, networks, tools, and credentials; it is released under the Apache 2.0 license and extensible to Arm and Intel chips.
  • Sentry is an out-of-band watchdog running on NVIDIA BlueField-4 DPUs that can quarantine an agent attempting to escape its boundary within milliseconds; the launch comes with backing from over 100 industry partners, including Anthropic.
  • SKAIA Lesson: Agent safety is becoming standardized infrastructure — adopt open guardrail tooling like OpenShell for SKAIA's client agent deployments so safety and permission enforcement are selling points, not custom engineering.

Source: Cybernews, Sept. 28, 2026

4. 자율 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

  • Instinct (Spear Street Technology Inc.), a San Francisco startup building a personal AI agent for everyday consumer tasks, announced on September 28, 2026 that it raised $1 billion in Series C funding at a $10 billion valuation.
  • The round included Sequoia Capital, Benchmark Capital, and Coatue — a fourfold valuation jump in a single month after a $250 million round at $2.5 billion in August 2026.
  • Instinct's agent acts rather than just chats: users text or call, and the agent uses its own phone number and computer to book travel, place calls for appointments, pay bills, cancel subscriptions, and order groceries; new features include a "concierge" phone-calling service and a "trusted person network" letting agents coordinate across users.
  • The autonomy requires deep access to personal data, and Instinct's early privacy policy drew scrutiny for overreach; the company has since updated it and says it now uses short-lived credentials, isolated sandboxes, and self-checks for hallucinations.
  • SKAIA Lesson: The $10B bet on action-taking agents validates the revenue-agent thesis — but consumer backlash on privacy shows permission boundaries and credential hygiene are the price of admission for any agent that touches a client's accounts.

Source: TechCrunch, Sept. 28, 2026

코헤시티, 에이전트 메모리·설정까지 백업하는 'Agent Resilience' 출시

  • At its Catalyst 2026 event, Cohesity introduced Agent Resilience, a backup and recovery capability for the infrastructure behind enterprise AI agents, launching with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and Bedrock Agents.
  • It protects the agent's own state — memory and configuration — along with the databases, file systems, and services agents interact with, so teams can restore an agent to a known-good point after memory corruption, misconfiguration, or malicious activity.
  • The feature is available now to select customers with general availability targeted for end of 2026; Microsoft and Google agent platforms are on the roadmap.
  • Cohesity's fifth annual Global Cyber Resilience Report (3,200 respondents across 12 countries) found that 56% of organizations are not well prepared to detect or contain unintended actions by AI agents.
  • SKAIA Lesson: For client-facing revenue agents, a known-good snapshot of agent state should be part of the SLA — back up the agent itself, not just its data, so one bad run cannot permanently poison a client's pipeline.

Source: Futurum Group, Sept. 28, 2026
