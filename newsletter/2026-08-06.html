4. 자율 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

미국, 오픈웨이트 AI 모델은 자율 안전테스트 대상에서 제외

  • The U.S. administration told major AI developers that its voluntary government safety-testing framework will not apply to open-weight models such as Meta's Llama and Nvidia's Nemotron.
  • The decision creates different oversight paths for closed frontier models and downloadable open models even as both become more capable.
  • The policy debate is moving from abstract AI safety into concrete questions about pre-release testing, cybersecurity capability, and who is accountable after deployment.
  • For autonomous-agent builders, government rules may vary, but internal permission boundaries, audit trails, and action verification remain necessary operational controls.

Source: Reuters, Aug. 4, 2026

세일즈포스, ‘모델보다 학습 루프가 자산’인 자가개선 에이전트 제시

  • Salesforce argues that durable agent value comes from a governed improvement loop around the model rather than from owning the frontier model itself.
  • The loop measures outcomes, diagnoses failures, tests changes to prompts, tools, retrieval, routing, and workflow, then keeps only improvements that pass evaluation.
  • Salesforce reports more than 11 million Agentforce calls per day, making manual tuning impractical at scale and increasing the value of automated evaluation.
  • The company also warns about reward hacking: a self-improving agent can optimize the wrong metric unless external evidence, regression tests, and human gates constrain persistence.
  • SKAIA takeaway: learning can be autonomous, but durable memory and policy changes should require verified evidence and rollback capability.

Source: Salesforce, Jul. 23, 2026

코덱스 사용 데이터가 보여준 ‘멀티 에이전트 업무’의 실제 확산

  • A large-scale study of Codex usage found that active users grew more than fivefold during the first half of 2026.
  • More than 10% of users manage three or more concurrent Codex agents in at least one week, while 26.6% use reusable skills for complex workflows.
  • The share of individual users delegating at least one task estimated to require more than eight hours of experienced human work increased nearly tenfold.
  • The evidence suggests that agentic AI is shifting from short assistance toward parallel delegation, reusable procedures, and long-running work.
  • SKAIA takeaway: durable state, budgets, specialist caps, and resumable operations become more important as delegation length and concurrency increase.

Source: The Shift to Agentic AI: Evidence from Codex, arXiv, Jun. 25, 2026

5. 마케팅 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

AI 검색이 구매 여정의 첫 단계로 급부상, 전년 대비 200% 성장

  • Salesforce reports that agentic search as the first step in a shopping journey grew 200% year over year.
  • Traffic referred from AI chats grew between 150% and 428% year over year in every measured quarter, based on Salesforce platform shopping data.
  • Only 28% of commerce organizations say they currently use agentic AI, while another 44% plan to adopt it within six months.
  • The marketing implication is significant: brands now need product information and content that AI assistants can understand before the customer ever reaches the brand's website.
  • SKAIA takeaway: future local-business acquisition should track AI-assisted discovery in addition to Google search, ads, social, and direct inquiries.

Source: Salesforce State of Commerce analysis, Jul. 28, 2026

허브스팟, 마케팅·영업 AI 팀을 한곳에 모은 Agent Hub 공개

  • HubSpot launched Agent Hub and Agent Builder in public beta for Professional and Enterprise customers.
  • Agent Hub shows agents across the customer journey with live status and recent results, organized around outcomes such as building demand, winning deals, delighting customers, and scaling growth.
  • Agent Builder lets teams describe a custom agent in plain language and build it directly on existing CRM contacts, deals, calls, and buying signals.
  • The product direction is moving away from isolated AI features toward a managed portfolio of agents that share business context and measurable outcomes.
  • SKAIA takeaway: the defensible layer is not the number of agents; it is the shared customer state, permissions, outcome tracking, and reusable workflow context underneath them.

Source: HubSpot, Jul. 23, 2026

마케팅 분석이 ‘사후 귀속’에서 ‘실시간 에이전트 최적화’로 이동

  • Salesforce is positioning marketing AI agents as an action layer that operates while campaigns are still live rather than only explaining performance after the fact.
  • Its Paid Media Optimization Agent can surface underperforming ads, recommend pauses, and let marketers act from the same performance workflow.
  • The marketer still owns strategy, budget, thresholds, and control, while the agent shortens the time between detecting a problem and taking action.
  • This changes attribution from a reporting discipline into a closed loop of measurement, recommendation, action, and new measurement.
  • SKAIA takeaway: a revenue agent should not only report conversion loss; it should propose a bounded next action, request approval when needed, execute once, and verify the resulting revenue event.

Source: Salesforce, Jul. 24, 2026
