AI 인프라 베팅 계속된다…Source Foundry에 4억달러 신규 투자

앨버타 10월19일 주민투표, 분리 문제를 포함한 중대 정치 변수로 부상

  • Elections Alberta confirms that a province-wide referendum is scheduled for October 19, 2026.
  • The first question asks voters whether Alberta should remain a province of Canada or whether the government should begin the constitutional legal process required to hold a later binding referendum on separation.
  • The October vote itself does not directly separate Alberta from Canada; it determines whether the government should move toward the legal process for a binding separation referendum.
  • For businesses, the issue matters because prolonged constitutional uncertainty can affect investment confidence, federal-provincial negotiations, energy policy, and long-term planning.

3. AI 스타트업 성공 스토리 — YouTube

무료 제품을 유통 엔진으로 바꿔 3천만달러 ARR까지 키운 Fathom

  • Fathom used a strong free AI meeting-notetaker as a customer-acquisition engine rather than treating free users only as a cost center.
  • Individual users naturally exposed the product to colleagues and meeting participants, creating a distribution loop that later expanded into paid team features.
  • The strategy shows how a free product can work when usage itself spreads the product and when paid collaboration, administration, and organizational value sit downstream.
  • SKAIA lesson: free offers should reveal a measurable revenue problem or create distribution; they should not become unlimited unpaid customization.

The approximately $30 million ARR figure comes from founder/interview material in the SKAIA YouTube research archive and is not independently audited here.

‘지루한’ HOA 시장을 5천만달러 사업으로 만든 Vantaca의 버티컬 전략

  • Vantaca focused deeply on HOA and community-association management instead of competing as a generic horizontal SaaS product.
  • The product became embedded in industry-specific operations, customer records, automation, and payment flows, making the value proposition harder to replace with a simple feature clone.

AI 에이전트를 ‘좌석’이 아닌 업무 결과로 팔아 고가 계약 만든 1Mind

  • 1Mind positions its GTM agents around continuous work such as qualification, demos, onboarding, and customer-success support instead of selling a single chatbot feature.

  • SKAIA lesson: price around completed revenue work only after the workflow is measurable, attributable, and repeatable enough to support outcome-based economics.

4. 자율 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

Claude Code, 승인 팝업 줄이는 Auto Mode를 기본값으로 전환

  • Anthropic says Claude Code's Auto Mode will become the default for Pro, Max, and Team accounts starting August 14.
  • Instead of asking for approval at every step, the agent proceeds automatically unless an action is judged irreversible, destructive, or directed outside the user's environment.
  • Anthropic reports that a 1,053-user test caught 89% of harmful actions with Auto Mode versus 13.6% with manual review, while manual users approved 97% of permission prompts.
  • SKAIA lesson: human approval is not automatically safer when users become habituated to prompts; risk-based approval plus hard-deny policies can be more reliable than approving everything manually.

Safety percentages are Anthropic-reported test results.

Source: TechCrunch — August 9, 2026

Cloudflare, 사람 아닌 AI 에이전트를 위한 전용 브라우저 Kitesurf 공개

  • Cloudflare launched Kitesurf, a cloud-hosted browser designed specifically for AI agents rather than human users.
  • The design prioritizes context management, token cost, scalability, browser automation, and a threat model that includes prompt injection instead of human-facing tabs and extensions.
  • Developers can use it to navigate websites, fill forms, and complete browser-based tasks without maintaining their own browser stack.
  • SKAIA lesson: browser automation is becoming infrastructure, but external web actions still need permission scopes, idempotency, evidence capture, and fallback paths.

Cloudflare's efficiency and 215,000-plus web-platform-test figures are vendor-reported.

Source: TechCrunch — August 7, 2026

Google Maps, 검색에서 주문·호텔·티켓까지 수행하는 생활형 에이전트로 진화

  • Google Maps' Ask Maps is adding agentic capabilities for food ordering, hotel discovery and booking handoffs, and event-ticket search.
  • Food orders can be assembled through partners such as Square, Toast, and Uber Eats, while hotel availability and prices can be compared before users complete bookings on partner sites.
  • Personal Intelligence can optionally use Gmail and Google Calendar context for more personalized answers and is off by default.
  • SKAIA lesson: the winning agent experience increasingly combines trusted personal context with a narrow real-world action and leaves high-risk transaction boundaries with the system of record.

Source: TechCrunch — August 6, 2026

5. 마케팅 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

리테일러들, AI 쇼핑 트래픽은 잡되 고객 데이터는 넘기지 않으려 한다

  • Retailers including Walmart, Ulta Beauty, and Wayfair are optimizing product information for ChatGPT and Gemini discovery while trying to keep checkout on their own sites.
  • Juniper Research expects AI-agent-referred shopping spend to reach about $8 billion this year, while Adobe says 41% of U.S. consumers used generative AI for online shopping in June.
  • Adobe also reports that AI-referred visitors generated 41% higher revenue per visit than traditional-channel visitors, and Ulta says AI-referred shoppers show roughly double the conversion and intent.
  • SKAIA lesson: AI discovery can be an acquisition channel, but the durable asset remains first-party customer data, transaction history, and the direct post-purchase relationship.

Shopping and conversion metrics are attributed to Juniper Research, Adobe Analytics, and retailer executives in Reuters reporting.

Source: Reuters — August 7, 2026

Klaviyo, AI 고객성공 스타트업 Agency 인수…마케팅·지원 에이전트 통합 가속

  • Klaviyo agreed to acquire Agency, a three-year-old AI-powered customer-success startup that had raised $32 million.
  • Agency founder Elias Torres will become Klaviyo's chief product officer, and the 25-person team will help expand Composer for campaign creation and Customer Agent for post-sale support.
  • Klaviyo says years of customer and commerce data can give its agents an advantage over general-purpose agent platforms.
  • SKAIA lesson: the strategic moat for marketing agents is shared customer context across acquisition, campaign execution, service, retention, and revenue—not an isolated content generator.

Deal terms were not disclosed; future scale ambitions are company statements.

Source: TechCrunch — August 5, 2026

업데이트 — Shopify, AI 검색 유입·주문 3배 증가…전통 검색과 공존 확인

  • Shopify says AI-driven traffic and orders to merchant stores tripled year over year in the second quarter.
  • The company argues that AI search is complementing rather than replacing traditional search, which still represents roughly one-third of storefront sessions.
  • Half of AI-referred sessions reportedly land directly on product-detail pages, compressing the journey from discovery to a high-intent product view.
  • SKAIA lesson: future attribution should track AI-assistant referrals as a distinct acquisition source while preserving the merchant's own conversion and customer-lifetime data.

Traffic and referral behavior metrics are Shopify-reported operating figures; the story is labeled as an update because AI-driven discovery growth was covered in the August 6 issue.
