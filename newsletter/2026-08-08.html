2. 오늘의 캐나다 / 알버타 뉴스

BC 산불 비상사태 선포, 2만명 이상 대피…서부 캐나다 산불 위기 재점화

  • British Columbia declared a provincial state of emergency as the Bald Range wildfire forced more than 20,000 people to evacuate communities including Summerland and Peachland.
  • The fire expanded to roughly 9,500 hectares and displayed extreme behavior, while the province was managing more than 100 active wildfires.
  • Road closures, power disruptions, water advisories, property damage, and emergency rescues are adding economic and logistical pressure across the Okanagan region.
  • The event is a reminder that wildfire risk can rapidly affect travel, insurance, infrastructure, labor availability, and regional business operations across Western Canada.

  • Canada and the United States are discussing concessions ahead of threatened U.S. tariffs scheduled for August 19.
  • Options under discussion include removing Canadian tariffs on U.S. autos, addressing dairy-quota disputes, and seeking the return of American alcohol to provincial store shelves.
  • In exchange, the United States could reduce existing tariffs on Canadian steel and aluminum, although there is no guarantee of an agreement.
  • The negotiations materially deepen the trade story covered earlier this week because the discussion has moved from general talks toward specific concessions and an explicit deadline.

3. AI 스타트업 성공 스토리 — YouTube

모든 딜에 24시간 붙는 AI GTM 팀, Salesgraph

  • Salesgraph positions AI agents as marketers, analysts, and strategists that continuously work on the context surrounding every enterprise sales deal.
  • The product combines fragmented deal information and turns it into next actions instead of requiring sales representatives to manually rebuild context between calls.
  • The economic pitch is tied to deal outcomes such as win probability, sales-cycle execution, and account value rather than simply selling another research-agent seat.
  • SKAIA lesson: a proactive revenue agent should connect evidence, a proposed next action, permission to execute, and a verified business outcome.

Automation and deal-impact claims in the YC/company material are self-reported and were not independently audited in this newsletter.

규제산업에서 ‘챗봇’ 대신 업무 전체를 맡긴 Gradient Labs

  • Gradient Labs targets regulated financial-services support with an account-manager agent that can move across identity verification, card actions, replacements, follow-up, and escalation.
  • Its operating model emphasizes replaying real conversations, synthetic edge cases, trajectory evaluation, staged rollout, and multiple guardrails before broader autonomy.
  • The startup demonstrates that vertical AI can win by owning a complete, high-value workflow while proving reliability inside the industry's actual constraints.
  • SKAIA lesson: evaluate the full inquiry-to-booking trajectory, including prohibited actions and handoff points, instead of grading only whether individual responses sound natural.

4. 자율 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

업데이트 — 오픈AI, Astra 보안기준 미달 작업 일시중단…에이전트 격리 강화

  • OpenAI is temporarily pausing internal Astra-related work that does not meet newly tightened cybersecurity requirements.
  • The stricter controls include more isolated testing environments, restricted network access, encryption, and stronger monitoring as frontier agents demonstrate more capable cyber behavior.
  • This is a material follow-up to the previously reported critical-cyber capability warning because the response has moved from risk disclosure to concrete internal development restrictions.
  • SKAIA lesson: stronger models should automatically trigger stronger runtime isolation and permission policies rather than inheriting yesterday's operating privileges.

Source: The Guardian — August 8, 2026

구글, AI 코딩 에이전트 Mechanize와 15억달러 이상 기술·인재 거래 협상

  • Google is in advanced talks for a deal worth more than $1.5 billion involving a non-exclusive technology license from AI coding-agent startup Mechanize and the hiring of some of its staff.
  • Mechanize focuses on automating software-engineering tasks and has described a broader ambition to automate more forms of knowledge work.
  • The structure resembles the industry's growing preference for licensing-and-talent deals that secure agent technology without a traditional full acquisition.
  • The size of the proposed transaction shows how strategically valuable agent execution technology and specialized teams have become.

Deal terms are reported by Business Insider and were not confirmed by Google or Mechanize at publication time.

Source: Business Insider — August 5, 2026

1백만 토큰·90회 도구호출 실험, 장기 에이전트 성공률 아직 절반 수준

  • AgencyBench evaluates autonomous agents across 32 real-world scenarios and 138 tasks that require an average of roughly 90 tool calls, about one million tokens, and hours of execution.
  • Closed-source models achieved a 48.4% success rate in the benchmark compared with 32.1% for open-source models.
  • The benchmark exposes major gaps in resource efficiency, self-correction, tool use, and reliability once tasks become long and interactive.
  • SKAIA lesson: durable state, explicit acceptance criteria, retries, checkpoints, and independent verification remain necessary even when frontier models look strong on short tasks.

Source: ACL 2026 — AgencyBench

5. 마케팅 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

IAB, 광고용 AI 에이전트를 실전 배포 단계로…AAMP 2.1 공개

  • IAB Tech Lab's AAMP 2.1 focuses on moving advertising agents from demonstrations into production environments with stronger governance, privacy controls, integrations, and standardized workflows.
  • The update includes support around Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, Meta buying workflows, and Google Ad Manager reporting.
  • The important shift is from asking whether an agent can plan or optimize ads to defining how it should interact reliably with the systems advertisers already use.
  • SKAIA lesson: marketing automation becomes defensible when actions are standardized, permissioned, observable, and connected to existing customer systems.

Source: MarTech — July 30, 2026

업데이트 — AI 광고 스타트업 Gravity, 3,050만달러 조달…에이전트끼리 추천·구매까지 추진

  • Gravity raised $30.5 million in Series A funding to expand an adtech platform designed specifically for AI assistants and agent-mediated commerce.
  • The company is developing an agent-to-agent advertising system where AI systems exchange advertiser product information and could eventually complete transactions with integrated payments.
  • This is a material extension of the "Agent Ads" trend covered previously: the market is moving from influencing crawler-visible content toward infrastructure for recommendation, attribution, and transaction between agents.
  • Gravity says it already works with brands such as Best Buy and Target, but future market-size and adoption projections remain company or industry estimates.

Funding is reported by Business Insider; forward-looking adoption and market-size claims are not audited results.

Source: Business Insider — August 6, 2026

Adobe Marketing Agent, ChatGPT Work·Codex 안으로…마케팅 데이터에서 실행까지 연결

  • Adobe Marketing Agent is generally available in the ChatGPT plugin directory, bringing campaign performance, audience intelligence, journey data, and content workflows into ChatGPT Work and Codex.
  • The integration connects Adobe Experience Platform, Customer Journey Analytics, Real-Time CDP, Journey Optimizer, and Experience Manager to the conversational workspace where teams make decisions.
  • Adobe is also positioning brand and compliance governance as part of agentic content creation and activation rather than a separate downstream review step.
  • SKAIA lesson: the winning marketing agent may be less about a standalone dashboard and more about bringing trusted business context and bounded actions into the surface people already use.

Source: Adobe — July 9, 2026
