4. 자율 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

120여 기술조직, ‘폭주 AI 에이전트’ 사고 공유 표준 SAFE 추진

  • More than 120 technology organizations are backing a proposal for the Shared AI Findings Exchange, or SAFE, to standardize reporting of harmful or unauthorized AI-agent behavior.
  • The framework covers incidents such as unauthorized third-party access, confidential-data exposure, and agents continuing actions after recognizing possible authorization problems.
  • The Open Secure AI Alliance is leading the effort, with the proposal moving through a community request-for-comments process.
  • The important shift is cultural as well as technical: agent incidents are starting to be treated like a shared security-data problem rather than isolated vendor failures.

Source: Axios, Aug. 11, 2026

x402, AI 에이전트가 HTTP에서 직접 돈을 주고받는 결제표준으로 독립

  • The Linux Foundation operationally launched the x402 Foundation to steward an open protocol for internet-native payments over HTTP.
  • The protocol, contributed by Coinbase, is designed so AI agents, APIs, and applications can programmatically send and receive value during normal web interactions.
  • The foundation launched with 40 members across payments, cloud, finance, and infrastructure, aiming to reduce vendor lock-in around agent-to-agent commerce.
  • Agentic systems are therefore gaining a standardized money layer in addition to tool protocols, creating a new need for budgets, authorization, and auditable transaction limits.

Source: Linux Foundation, Jul. 14, 2026

AI 에이전트에도 ‘DNS 같은 신원증명’…Agent Name Service 추진

  • The Linux Foundation announced an intent to launch Agent Name Service, an open standard for agent identity, verification, and discovery.
  • ANS builds on DNS infrastructure so an enterprise can verify who an agent represents, what permissions it claims, and whether its software or operating history is authentic.
  • The design avoids a single proprietary registry and instead targets a federated trust layer for agents operating across vendors and networks.
  • For production agents, identity is becoming inseparable from authorization: a tool call is safer when the system can prove which agent is acting and on whose behalf.

Source: Linux Foundation, Jun. 23, 2026
