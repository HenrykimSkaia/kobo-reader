  • Trellis builds agents that run short-term-rental operations across guest messaging, cleaning, maintenance, vendors, costs and owner updates.
  • The core product idea is unresolved-state ownership: the agent keeps operational context and follows an issue from first message through resolution and reporting instead of handing fragments between tools.
  • Trellis says it reached about $500,000 ARR across 35 customers and 3,000 listings in two months, with roughly 15% weekly growth.

정적 안전테스트의 한계…AgenticEval은 시험 자체를 계속 진화시킨다

  • AgenticEval reframes AI safety evaluation as a continuous, self-evolving process rather than a one-time static benchmark.
  • The multi-agent framework ingests policy documents, generates tests and then creates harder cases based on previous evaluation results.
  • In the paper’s EU AI Act experiment, GPT-5’s measured safety rate fell from 72.5% to 36.36% as the evaluation evolved, showing how static tests can overestimate robustness.
  • SKAIA lesson: evaluator quality must improve with the system; a passing test suite should not become permanent proof of safety.

Source · ACL Findings 2026 · Open paper

웹 에이전트 9종 모두 취약점 노출…SecureWebArena가 보여준 현실

  • SecureWebArena evaluates web agents across six realistic environments and 2,970 adversarial trajectories.
  • The benchmark covers both user-level manipulation and environmental attacks instead of testing prompts in isolation.
  • Experiments across nine representative vision-language models found universal vulnerabilities to subtle manipulation and meaningful security-performance trade-offs.
  • SKAIA lesson: safe browser automation needs environment-aware defenses and trajectory-level verification, not only prompt filtering.

AI 에이전트로 A/B 테스트를 미리 돌릴 수 있을까…67개 실제 실험으로 검증

  • A new framework tests whether AI agents can simulate marketing A/B-test outcomes before companies spend real traffic, engineering time and weeks of experimentation.
  • Across 67 historical marketing tests, the baseline simulation captured directional signal with 0.70 sign overlap but systematically exaggerated effect sizes.
  • A two-phase calibration process reduced squared prediction error by roughly 77 times, while a within-subject design reduced standard errors by about 2.4 times.
  • The authors emphasize limits: agent simulations can help screen ideas, but they are not yet a replacement for real customer experiments.

Source · arXiv · Aug 3, 2026 · Open paper
