1. 오늘의 AI 뉴스

트럼프, 정보국장 제이 클레이튼을 AI 차르로 임명… '슈퍼 인텔리전스 포스' 신설

  • President Donald Trump on Sunday named Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton as the administration's AI czar and head of the newly created "Super Intelligence Force."

  • SKAIA Lesson: The federal layer is moving from voluntary pledges toward coordinated oversight with a hard 120-day report deadline — agents for local shops should be built with permission controls, human approval steps, and auditable behavior now, not retrofitted later.

Source: Associated Press (via WVLT), Oct. 4, 2026

2. 오늘의 캐나다 / 알버타 뉴스

캐나다 9월 고용 서프라이즈 — 6만 개 증가, 실업률 7.1% 유지

  • Statistics Canada's September labour force survey, released Oct. 2, showed a net gain of 60,000 jobs, far above economists' expectations of 5,000.
  • Gains were concentrated in full-time work (+106,000), more than offsetting a drop in part-time employment, and rebounded from losses of more than 100,000 jobs over the previous two months.
  • The unemployment rate held steady at 7.1%, while youth unemployment (ages 15–24) rose to 14.7%, a 15-year high outside the pandemic years, per StatCan.
  • Trade-sensitive manufacturing added 28,000 jobs — its first gain since January — and Alberta led all provinces with a 42,500-job increase, which BMO's Doug Porter called Alberta's largest-ever single-month gain outside pandemic-era volatility.
  • SKAIA Lesson: A resilient Alberta labour market supports local-shop spending power — the exact customer base Foloup targets — but the 14.7% youth jobless rate is a reminder that the recovery is uneven.

Source: Investment Executive, Oct. 2, 2026

앨버타 주유세 13¢/L 면제 시행 — 그런데 캘거리 펌프값은 오히려 상승

  • Alberta's full suspension of its provincial fuel tax — 13 cents per litre on gasoline and diesel — took effect Oct. 1 and runs through Dec. 31, 2026, after WTI averaged $90.54 during the Aug. 18–Sept. 15 review period (threshold: $80).
  • The province estimated the cut would bring Alberta's average pump price to about $1.62 a litre (from 175.5 cents on Sept. 21), the lowest in Canada.
  • But by Saturday Oct. 3, CityNews reported via GasBuddy that some Calgary stations were charging $1.70–$1.80 per litre, as rising crude costs offset the tax cut.
  • The government said it will monitor pump prices for gouging; businesses found guilty face court fines of up to $300,000 or up to two years of jail time.
  • SKAIA Lesson: When input costs erase policy relief overnight, local shops feel the squeeze first — cost pressure is the opening for an agent that protects margins through follow-up and reactivation instead of more ad spend.

Source: Government of Alberta (via Electrical Industry News Week), Oct. 1, 2026

공항 민영화 반대 여론 여전 — Nanos 조사, 캘거리 공항 포함

  • Prime Minister Mark Carney's proposal to bring private operators into Toronto Pearson, Montreal, Calgary and Vancouver airports under long-term leases remains unpopular, a new Nanos Research survey for CTV News found.
  • 47% of respondents said they oppose or somewhat oppose private control of the major airports, versus 38% who support it; 15% were unsure (survey of 1,057 Canadians, Sept. 27–29, margin of error ±3.0 points).

3. AI 스타트업 성공 스토리 — YouTube

3명으로 3,000만 달러 ARR에 도전하는 '자율 비즈니스' Swan AI

  • Swan AI is a GTM automation platform built by Amos Bar-Joseph (two prior B2B SaaS exits) around an "autonomous business" bet: scale with intelligence, not headcount — publicly targeting $30M ARR with a tiny team and a fleet of AI agents.
  • The company reached seven figures in ARR (founder-reported) with 200+ customers and, at one point, zero employees beyond the three founders — $1M ARR in 9 weeks with no sales team and no paid ads.
  • It wins customers by dogfooding: Swan's own go-to-market runs on its platform — AI agents detect ICPs in real time, run research/outreach/follow-up inside Slack, and turned LinkedIn into a pipeline engine; one inbound process runs 30,000 times a month.
  • Swan makes money as B2B SaaS (GTM automation subscriptions); the no-hire constraint became the growth system — "scaling employees rather than scaling the org chart."
  • The one-year update: sales was the first thing that broke, and the team doubled from 3 to 6 — "AI can do the work, but scaling is decisions, and decisions stay human."
  • SKAIA Lesson: The constraint-first playbook maps directly to Foloup's pilots: cap headcount and cost, automate the follow-up workflow end to end, and measure ARR per employee — the shops that survive will be the ones whose operations scale with intelligence, not staff.
  • Revenue/customer figures are SELF-REPORTED by the founder in interviews and are not independently verified.

4. 자율 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

애플, AI 에이전트 위험을 이유로 macOS 'Full Disk Access' 권한 강화

  • On October 2, 2026, Apple announced via Apple Developer News ("Updates to Full Disk Access in macOS") that it will add controls so users can grant Full Disk Access only through "very explicit user action."
  • Apple said some developers use the permission in ways that expose files, mail, messages, and browsing history "without users' full knowledge and understanding."
  • Apple's stated reason is AI agents: as they become more capable and autonomous, "the risks associated with this level of access will grow substantially."
  • The move follows reports that Meta's Muse Mac app read a journalist's private messages (a claim Meta disputed) and a prior Wired report on a ChatGPT Mac app flaw — context TechCrunch also reported; Apple named no app.
  • Apple did not say which macOS release will carry the change or when it ships; this is a policy direction, not an immediate settings flip.
  • SKAIA Lesson: As agents get more autonomy, platforms force explicit, granular consent. Foloup's booking/follow-up agents should keep every sensitive action (booking, customer data access, outbound messages) behind explicit confirmation rather than a blanket permission.

Source: AI Tech Daily, Oct. 2, 2026

DigitalOcean, AI 에이전트 인프라 번들 'Agent Droplets' 월 50달러부터 출시

  • On October 1, 2026, DigitalOcean announced Agent Droplets, a single monthly subscription bundling everything an agent needs: managed runtime, governed tool access, serverless inference, persistent memory, and storage.
  • Two tiers: Pro at $50/month (15% discount on included agent resources) and Team at $200/month (20% discount); customers choose whether spending stops at the allowance or continues at list prices.
  • The bundle includes Harness Runtime (dedicated microVM sandboxes that pause when idle), an Inference Engine (company-hosted open models; Claude and GPT remain pay-as-you-go), and Action Gateway with governed access to 16,000+ tools via a single managed endpoint.
  • Unlimited agents with no per-seat charges; enterprise security (SSO, MFA, role-based access, audit logs) included on every tier, plus a new prepaid "Inference and Agents Balance" ($5–$500).
  • SKAIA Lesson: Agent infrastructure is commoditizing into predictable small-business pricing — the same "one subscription for everything the agent needs" model SKAIA's local-shop pilots need to compete against; hosting cost is becoming a feature, not a moat. Customer data and verified booking outcomes remain the defensible layer.

Source: Business Wire (DigitalOcean press release), Oct. 1, 2026

Photon, 앱 대신 메신저에서 돌아가는 AI 에이전트 인프라로 450만 달러 시드 유치

  • Startup Photon raised $4.5 million in seed funding, co-led by Gradient and A*, with participation from Vercel, HongShan, Z Fellows, Llama Ventures, and Karman.
  • Photon's thesis: users no longer want to install new apps — they want agents reachable through messaging apps they already use (iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS/RCS, email, voice). It offers a unified API, channel framework, CLI, and observability suite.
  • The company reports over 40,000 developer sign-ups, 10x revenue growth in four months (company-reported; revenue figures not disclosed), messaging volume up 5x in the last month, and churn under 3%.
  • The open-source version still accounts for 98% of usage; the hosted platform (99.95% uptime, SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA-compliant) added paying customers in fintech, consumer AI, and other verticals including Corgi Insurance and Rho.
  • Co-founder/CEO Daniel Tian held an actual "app funeral" in San Francisco on September 17 (coffin of app icons, panels from Vercel, Stripe, OpenAI) and told TechCrunch the shift from apps to agents is "directionally, inevitable," while noting "it's going to take a while."
  • SKAIA Lesson: Foloup is already betting exactly where Photon validated developer demand: the agent lives in the message thread, not in another app download. Photon's 40,000-developer pull is a distribution signal that "agents inside conversations" is where the next wave of small-business automation will be adopted.

Source: TechCrunch, Oct. 1, 2026

5. 마케팅 AI 에이전트 관련 뉴스

컨스트럭터, 대화형 AI 에이전트 도입 900% 증가로 MACH 어워드 수상

  • Constructor, an ecommerce product-discovery platform, won the 2026 MACH Impact Award in the "Agentic Achievement" category for production AI agent deployments across retail sites.
  • The company reported a 900% year-over-year increase in the number of companies deploying its conversational AI agents (company-reported; the baseline count was not disclosed).
  • Its agentic portfolio covers three agent types: a shopper-facing AI Shopping Agent, an AI Product Insights Agent that answers questions on product pages, and a Merchant Intelligence Agent that investigates discovery performance and automates merchandising work — plus a Discovery MCP server exposing its commerce intelligence to external engines like ChatGPT.
  • Across customer deployments, the company reported (company-reported): a 57% reduction in support requests, shoppers converting at more than double the rate of standard search, and 52% higher add-to-cart and 56% higher purchase rates among shoppers using a Constructor agent.
  • SKAIA Lesson: Agents that live inside the shop's existing stack (site search, merchandising tools) and are measured on conversion lift are the credible revenue story — Foloup should pitch the same "measured booking lift on your own tools" frame, not agent novelty.

Source: PRNewswire (Constructor press release), Oct. 2, 2026

드라이브센트릭, 딜러십 차량 매입용 AI 에이전트 '어콰이어 에이전트' 공개

  • DriveCentric, the dealer industry's AI engagement platform, announced Acquire Agent — its latest native AI agent, built on the company's own 15 years of AI work (company-reported), focused on vehicle acquisition for car dealerships.
  • The agent engages customers immediately after they receive a trade valuation, picking up where their experience left off with personalized outreach and moving the conversation toward an appointment — it does not re-calculate the valuation, it acts on the intent signal.
  • Each agent runs on the same customer data, workflows, and permissions dealership teams already use; managers can train it on the store's own sales process, coach it when they want something handled differently, and audit every conversation across the store.
  • It is part of a lifecycle strategy: a Service-to-Sales Agent finds sales opportunities in the service drive, while Acquire Agent converts trade-valuation intent into acquisition appointments. Early Access begins October 15, with general availability December 1, 2026.
  • SKAIA Lesson: This is Foloup's exact thesis validated inside the industry Henry knows best — the agent's job is to never let a raised hand (a valuation request, an estimate inquiry) sit unanswered: intent-to-appointment, fully auditable by the shop.

Source: Used Car News, Oct. 3, 2026

Braze, AI 의사결정·캠페인 검수 에이전트 3종 공개…10월 순차 정식 출시

  • Customer engagement platform Braze announced three AI products at its Forge conference in Las Vegas: BrazeAI Decisioning Studio Go, Agentic Standards, and Operator Connect.
  • Decisioning Studio Go offers self-service AI decisioning for individualized customer journeys — initially targeting email performance (creative, subject lines, CTAs, imagery, send time) — and reaches general availability on Oct. 14, 2026.
  • Agentic Standards delegates campaign QA to autonomous LLM agents that audit entire journeys against brand standards, accessibility rules, and regulatory compliance, producing an audit log and auto-executing corrections; GA expected October 2026. Sportsbet's CRM team said (company-cited customer quote) it already caught missing unsubscribe links and eligibility-segment gaps.
  • Operator Connect brings Braze's Operator assistant into external AI environments (Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot) via the Model Context Protocol, so marketers can, for example, ask Claude to build a campaign structure directly inside Braze — with every action authenticated under the user's identity and governed by their Braze permissions.
  • SKAIA Lesson: Braze's pattern — agentic actions executed under the business's own permissions and approval flows — is exactly what makes agents trustworthy for local shops. Foloup should mirror it: approval gates plus permission-scoped actions, marketed as the safety story.

Source: MarTech, Oct. 1, 2026
