The Last Lantern of Greyhaven

Volume I — The City That Forgot the Sky

Greyhaven floats above a memory-eating mist, protected by seven ancient lanterns. When the seventh light dies, fourteen-year-old mapmaker Rowan Vale receives a compass from his missing mother—and discovers that his city was built to hide the road to an impossible sky.
